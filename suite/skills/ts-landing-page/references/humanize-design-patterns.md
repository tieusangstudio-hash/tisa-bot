# Humanize Design Patterns

> The details that make a page feel made-by-a-person, not generated. Layered on top of the anti-slop checklist: after removing the AI tells, add these human touches.

## 1. Intentional Imperfection

Machine-perfect symmetry reads as fake. Add controlled irregularity:
- Asymmetric layouts (60/40, offset visuals) instead of dead-center everything.
- Slight rotation or overlap on a card/image/badge (a few degrees) for a crafted feel.
- Vary card counts and sizes — a 2 + 1 large layout beats 3 identical boxes.
- Uneven-but-deliberate spacing rhythm, not uniform machine spacing.

Keep it *controlled* — the goal is "designed by a human", not "broken".

## 2. Texture Over Flat Void

Flat, empty backgrounds feel like a template. Add subtle depth:
- Faint noise/grain texture, soft gradient mesh, or a subtle pattern in one area.
- Layered shadows (soft, realistic) instead of one flat drop shadow everywhere.
- A background shape or blob behind the hero for depth — muted, not neon.

## 3. Local & Concrete Specificity

Generic = AI. Specific = real. Bake in Vietnamese reality:
- Real names, districts, and cities (not "John Doe" / "City").
- VND prices with realistic numbers (sourced, not invented).
- Business registration (MST), real address, hotline/Zalo number.
- Local proof: reviews with Vietnamese names + photos, screenshots of real messages.

## 4. Trust Signals That Look Earned

- Testimonials with **name + photo + specific result**, not "Great product! – A customer".
- Real logos, real numbers ("1.200+ học viên", not "many customers").
- Founder/team photo and a human sentence — a real face builds trust.
- Contact that a real business has: phone, Zalo, address, hours.

## 5. Natural Micro-Interactions

- Hover states: color/opacity shifts (not layout-shifting scale transforms).
- Gentle scroll reveals — subtle, not everything-flies-in.
- Transitions 150-300ms; nothing instant, nothing sluggish.
- One or two tasteful motion accents, not motion on every element.

## 6. Copy That Sounds Like a Person

- Contractions, direct address, a real voice — not corporate monotone.
- Occasional short fragment for emphasis. Like this.
- Specifics and small concrete details a human would mention.
- If VoC data exists, mirror the customer's actual phrasing.

## Application Order

1. Draft the page (structure per `conversion-structure-principles.md`).
2. Run `anti-ai-slop-checklist.md` — remove the tells.
3. Apply these humanize patterns — add the human touches.
4. Final export gate.

The result: on-brand, accessible, converts — and doesn't smell like AI.
